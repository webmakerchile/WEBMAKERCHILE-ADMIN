WebMakerChile is a content management platform for video creators. We use Login Kit to authenticate the content creator via TikTok OAuth, obtaining user.info.basic to display their TikTok profile in our admin dashboard. We use Content Posting API with video.publish scope to upload recorded videos directly from our platform to TikTok as private posts (SELF_ONLY), allowing the creator to review before making them public.
