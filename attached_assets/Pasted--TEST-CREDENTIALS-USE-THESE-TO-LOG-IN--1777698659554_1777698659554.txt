ABOUT THE APP
WebMaker Latam is a content management platform that
helps small businesses in Latin America create, edit
and publish vertical short-form videos to TikTok and
other social networks from a single dashboard.

LOGIN KIT (scope: user.info.basic)
Authenticates the TikTok creator and shows their
display name and avatar in the dashboard so the user
can confirm which TikTok account is connected.

CONTENT POSTING API (scope: video.upload)
Uploads finished videos as DRAFTS to the connected
TikTok account using push_by_file. The creator then
opens TikTok and publishes the draft. We do not use
direct posting.

END-TO-END FLOW (this is what the demo video shows):
1. Log in to admin.webmakerlatam.com with the test
   credentials shown above.
2. Open the "Studio" section and upload or record
   a vertical 1080x1920 video.
3. Generate AI cover and AI description for the video.
4. Open the "Cuentas" section and click
   "Conectar TikTok".
5. Approve the TikTok OAuth screen requesting
   user.info.basic and video.upload.
6. The connected TikTok account appears with its
   display name and avatar.
7. Open the video in "Videos" and click
   "Publicar en TikTok".
8. The video is uploaded as a draft via the Content
   Posting API. The creator then opens the TikTok
   app and publishes it.

WHY EACH SCOPE IS NEEDED
- user.info.basic: required so users see which TikTok
  account they connected (UX clarity, prevents wrong
  account).
- video.upload: required to transfer produced videos
  to TikTok as drafts. This is the core value of
  the integration.
